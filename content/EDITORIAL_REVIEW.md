## Revision status

All 335 authored lessons received a lesson-by-lesson editorial reading pass on September 20, 2026. The pass covered routed explanations, worked examples, local vocabulary, visible formative activities, takeaways, and source relevance. Corrections include squared bias, a threshold-classifier capacity assumption, signed state decay, sampling with replacement, repeated token positions, and option-order-dependent feedback. Expanded examples connect abstract mechanisms to inspectable arithmetic and everyday decisions. Dated model cases were checked against their named primary reports; this was not a fresh verification of every external link or a reproduction of model training.

The [lesson review record](LESSON_REVIEW.md) lists every reviewed asset, its specific finding, and remaining release requirements. The [teaching standards](AUTHORING.md#when-a-lesson-leaves-draft) define completion separately from word count, catalog coverage, and personal-study mastery. A reading pass is an editorial milestone, not independent subject validation or learner testing.

The existing revised question banks remain intact, including retired response contracts. This pass fixes misleading feedback but does not claim to have revalidated or replaced every assessment variant. Bare yes/no pairs, thin retake banks, generic reading destinations, and question-to-objective alignment still need systematic assessment review. Improve distractors by teaching plausible mistakes, not by padding answers to manipulate these statistics. New examples extend the explanations without silently claiming old evidence proves additional skills.

The lists below are prompts for the next targeted review. A short comparison can teach its stated scope well; a long page can still be confusing. Check learner explanations and transfers to fresh cases before deciding whether to expand, split, or retain a lesson.
