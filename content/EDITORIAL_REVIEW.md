## Revision status

The opening eleven lessons now define everyday roles before training vocabulary. Arithmetic, functions, data, learning methods, prediction, and neurons have expanded worked instruction; selected attention, flow/energy, associative-memory, and MoE lessons have added traces. The opening banks offer four active variants per family, with changed-number input fields in arithmetic, functions, prediction, and neurons. The latest revision expands all eight history and eight computing lessons plus algebra, matrices, derivatives, and the chain rule. These twenty lessons add eight active variants each, with intermediate numerical checks where the mechanism calls for calculation. Relevant computing foundations precede system-specific sequences, and a pixel experiment separates stored values from interpretation. Earlier checks remain readable to the backup validator and cannot be reused as fresh credit.

The system-path revision adds fifteen scoped routes and three worked synthesis projects for video, speech/audio, and robotics. Each new project includes a supplied paper fixture, a reference trace, a self/peer-review rubric, and eight fresh conceptual/numerical variants. The projects do not grade unseen artifacts or replace deeper mechanism lessons. Required prerequisites were checked against the selected reference outcomes; alternative architectures remain discoverable as extensions.

Most later lessons still require substantive editorial expansion and assessment revision. This pass does not claim that the corpus-wide assessment patterns have been eliminated. Use plausible mistakes tied to the taught mechanism; do not add universal-sounding wording to correct answers merely to manipulate these statistics. Numeric examples suit computational mechanisms, while historical and social comparisons need concrete cases and justified reasoning rather than forced arithmetic.
